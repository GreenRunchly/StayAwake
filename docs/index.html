Keep your Mac awake.
Right from the menu bar.

Stop your Mac from falling asleep during downloads, presentations, or long tasks—all from one simple icon in your menu bar.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

What It Does

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

One-Click Awake

Click the icon once to keep your screen and Mac awake. Click again to turn it off—no password needed.

Keep Running with Lid Closed

Right-click the icon to stop your Mac from sleeping even when you close your laptop lid.

Cooler & Quieter Mac

Optionally turn off Intel Turbo Boost from the menu to reduce heat and fan noise on Intel MacBooks.
